# Whispers are an Effect of the Haunting

The Whispral's breathing was a sound of each Whispral, so two Whisprals holding one Victim breathed twice over. Its replacement, the Whispers, is an Effect instead: it starts and stops with the Haunting, alongside Vanish and the Hallucinations, so one Whisper plays at a time however many Whisprals hold the Victim. A second Whispral attaching changes nothing; the Whisper playing fades out only when the last one lets go.

## Consequences

The Whisper sounds belong to the Haunting, not to the Whispral prefab: they load from the mod's bundle on their own, and no rule of a single Whispral (its jumps) applies to them.
